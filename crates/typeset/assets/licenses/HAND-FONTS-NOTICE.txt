The hand-drawn fonts (GelPen, GelPen Serif, Vanilla Extract)

These fonts are not covered by the AGPL. They are by Shara Weber and remain under her terms. They are included only so
Drafft.ink can display them. For any other use, get them from sharasfonts.com and follow her terms.

They are not part of the packages on crates.io. The files live in the Git repository of Drafft.ink
(crates/typeset/assets/fonts/GelPen*.ttf and VanillaExtract.ttf) and in the web app on https://drafft.ink. The Cargo
feature `hand-fonts` of drafftink-typeset embeds them and only builds from the repository. A build without the feature
(everything installed from crates.io) draws those three families with Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see the
other files in this folder).

The status of the licence is tracked in ATTRIBUTION.md of the repository.
